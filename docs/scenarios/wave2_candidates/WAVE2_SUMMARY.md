1. `scenario_01_relationship_truth.md` - The Sunday Soft Launch: a creator breakup looks like Mara moved on too fast, but receipts show Jace reversed the public timeline for sympathy and career momentum.
2. `scenario_02_friend_group_betrayal.md` - The Empty Fifth Chair: a friend group frames Rhea as a private-chat leaker, while cross-app receipts point to Mina using the fallout to protect a solo hosting opportunity.

7. `scenario_07_platform_adjacent_individual.md` - The Quiet Boost: a creator's reach panic looks like platform-adjacent retaliation, but her scheduler Mina changed launch settings for personal benefit.
8. `scenario_08_status_sabotage.md` - Empty Chair Energy: Priya looks like she skipped a community showcase, but receipts show Tess redirected venue updates and took the spotlight.

## Strongest By Hook

`scenario_01_relationship_truth.md` and `scenario_04_creator_feud_staged_scandal.md` have the strongest immediate hooks. Both start with high-legibility creator drama, visible emotional stakes, and a natural reason to check one more app.

## Strongest By Receipt Graph

`scenario_02_friend_group_betrayal.md` is strongest by receipt graph. Its timing, crop, source, motive, and role-reversal receipts create a clear bundle rather than a single deciding artifact.

## Strongest By Doomscroll Pull

`scenario_04_creator_feud_staged_scandal.md` has the strongest doomscroll pull because creator-feud content naturally travels across clip reactions, public discourse, story posts, and leaked private context.

## Strongest By Production Feasibility

`scenario_08_status_sabotage.md` looks most production-feasible. It can be carried by stills, text posts, a simple controlled clip, and private-message screenshots without relying on difficult visual continuity.

## Weakest Scenario

`scenario_08_status_sabotage.md` is the weakest as a distinct candidate despite its production strength, because its empty-chair social embarrassment overlaps too closely with `scenario_02_friend_group_betrayal.md`. It may still be valuable if the team wants a lower-production school/work/community MVP, but it needs a more differentiated hook.

## Recurring Risks Noticed

- Several scenarios use public screenshot or clip cropping; future revisions should vary the core transformation patterns.
- Creator-world and friend-group scenarios are currently strongest, but they risk becoming interchangeable if voice, cast, and app texture are not differentiated.
- Blink receipts carry high risk of feeling too convenient; they should rarely function as direct explanations.
- App unlocks often depend on comments mentioning another app; later waves should make some unlocks emerge from profile links, visual handles, repost chains, and saved screenshots.
- Timeline contradictions are common and useful, but they must stay legible without making the scenario feel like a timestamp puzzle.
- Responsible parties need early but non-obvious presence so the report cannot be brute-forced from the cast list.
- Production plans should avoid making small visual details carry required receipt meaning.
