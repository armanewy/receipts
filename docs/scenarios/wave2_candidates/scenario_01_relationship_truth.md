# Scenario 01: The Sunday Soft Launch

## 1. Scenario Metadata

- Title: The Sunday Soft Launch
- Category: Relationship truth / breakup ambiguity
- Tone: Messy creator breakup, public sympathy spiral, private timing mismatch
- Estimated playtime: 25-35 minutes
- Intended difficulty: Medium
- Starting app: Loop
- Apps used: Loop, Frame, Threadline, Blink
- Primary transformation types: Temporal inversion, selective cropping, source laundering, soft-launch contradiction, public/private mismatch, incentive trail
- One-sentence semantic promise: A viral soft-launch accusation looks like Mara moved on too fast, but the saved receipts show Jace shaped the timeline after ending the relationship privately.

## 2. Surface Event Semantics

- Artifact type: Viral Loop clip with a reaction chain and pinned comments.
- Emotional charge: Betrayal, embarrassment, judgment, and "she moved on before he could breathe" discourse.
- Apparent target: Mara Kim, half of a familiar creator couple.
- Immediate question: Did Mara soft-launch someone new right after Jace Bell posted a breakup clip?
- Why a user keeps scrolling: The clip is short, the comments keep naming other apps, and people argue over whether the orange jacket belongs to a new partner.

## 3. Public Interpretation Semantics

- Apparent actor: Mara.
- Apparent motive: She wanted to replace Jace publicly while keeping the couple audience.
- Apparent timeline: Jace posts heartbreak on Sunday night, then a fresh clip shows Mara leaving a studio in another person's jacket a few hours later.
- Apparent wronged party: Jace.
- Viral supporting artifact: A cropped Loop clip of Mara leaving Studio 6 in an orange jacket, paired with Jace's tearful breakup clip.
- Emotional reason it spreads: The feed gets a clean villain, a sad ex, and a visual symbol that feels easy to judge.
- What it gets right: Mara and Jace were already privately split, and the public posts were delayed.
- What it gets wrong: The breakup happened before the viral weekend, the jacket was not a romantic soft launch, and Jace helped make the reversed timeline spread.

## 4. Actual Causal Chain

- Responsible individual: Jace Bell.
- Motive/pressure: He wanted sympathy and solo momentum before a creator showcase announcement, while avoiding questions about why he ended the relationship privately before their queued couple content finished posting.
- Action taken: He nudged a fan account with a cropped older Loop clip, then posted his breakup clip after the fan thread was already moving.
- Manipulation/concealment method: He used an old cropped studio exit, let the fan account make it look current, and kept private breakup timing off the public feed.
- True timeline:
  - Thursday night: Jace ends the relationship over Blink and asks Mara to keep the split quiet until Monday because a Frame carousel is already queued.
  - Friday morning: Mara posts a vague Frame story saying the timing is "not mine to post yet."
  - Saturday afternoon: Mara rehearses at Studio 6 with stylist Sol Aris, who lends her the orange jacket.
  - Sunday evening: A fan Threadline account posts the soft-launch claim before Jace's public breakup clip goes live.
  - Monday: A solo creator showcase adds Jace to a visible slot after the sympathy wave.
- Intended public belief: Mara overlapped the relationship and embarrassed Jace.
- Unintended trace left behind: The fan thread timestamp comes before Jace's public clip, Sol's Blink snap explains the jacket, and Jace's private timing request conflicts with his public heartbreak timing.
- Why this explanation is more satisfying: It explains why the public timeline feels too neat, why the jacket clue collapses, why Mara stayed vague, and why Jace benefited from the sympathy wave.

## 5. Transformation Types Used

- Temporal inversion: The feed treats Jace's breakup clip as the cause and Mara's studio clip as the reaction, but the breakup happened days earlier and the fan thread started before Jace's public post.
- Selective cropping: The viral Loop clip hides Sol and the rehearsal context around the orange jacket.
- Source laundering: A fan account makes Jace's preferred story look like independent audience discovery.
- Soft-launch contradiction: The supposed romantic jacket clue is contradicted by Sol's own Blink snap and Frame studio context.
- Public/private mismatch: Jace's public heartbreak timing conflicts with his Blink request to delay the announcement.
- Incentive trail: The showcase slot makes the sympathy narrative useful to Jace.

## 6. App Semantic Roles

### Loop

- Role in scenario: Delivers the viral hook and the misleading visual shorthand.
- Key artifact: Cropped Studio 6 exit clip plus Jace's breakup reaction clip.
- Misleading surface: Mara appears to be leaving with a new partner's jacket after Jace posts heartbreak.
- Truth it reveals: The original remix chain shows the studio clip is older and connected to Sol's rehearsal styling.
- Unlock trigger: Comments tell players to check Mara's Frame story and the fan Threadline timeline.
- Adversarial concern: The jacket must not become a tiny-pixel dependency; the clip should make Sol's context findable through captions and comments.

### Frame

- Role in scenario: Shows relationship timing, queued content, and social context around the studio day.
- Key artifact: Mara's "not mine to post yet" story and the queued couple carousel.
- Misleading surface: Mara looks vague and image-managing.
- Truth it reveals: The relationship was already privately over, and public silence came from a timing agreement.
- Unlock trigger: A Frame story screenshot includes a Blink contact preview from Jace.
- Adversarial concern: Vague breakup posts can feel too generic unless they are anchored by specific timestamps and queued-post context.

### Threadline

- Role in scenario: Shows how the public interpretation spread and where the source chain wobbles.
- Key artifact: The first soft-launch thread from @softwatcher.
- Misleading surface: The fan thread looks like organic audience analysis.
- Truth it reveals: The thread predates Jace's public clip and repeats phrasing from Jace's draft captions.
- Unlock trigger: The thread links back to the Loop clip and quotes Frame screenshots.
- Adversarial concern: Text discourse can become too explicit; the account should feel like a fan amplifier, not a narrator.

### Blink

- Role in scenario: Provides private timing context without solving everything alone.
- Key artifact: Jace's delay request and Sol's jacket snap.
- Misleading surface: Cropped private screenshots initially look like Mara doing damage control.
- Truth it reveals: Jace knew the split was private before Sunday, and the jacket was Sol's styling piece.
- Unlock trigger: Frame screenshots and Threadline replies mention saved Blink snaps.
- Adversarial concern: The Jace message should confirm timing, not directly explain his whole plan.

## 7. Progressive App Disclosure

- Starting app and why: Loop starts with the most emotionally sticky artifact, a short clip that invites immediate judgment.
- Unlock 2 trigger: Loop comments reference Mara's Frame story from Friday and the queued couple carousel.
- Unlock 3 trigger: Frame captions and comments point to the @softwatcher Threadline timeline.
- Unlock 4 trigger: A Frame screenshot and a Threadline reply show cropped Blink messages that players can open in context.
- What question each unlock answers:
  - Frame answers whether the public breakup timing matches Mara's own posting.
  - Threadline answers who amplified the soft-launch claim and when.
  - Blink answers what Mara and Jace privately agreed before the public posts.
- What new question each unlock creates:
  - Frame creates the question of why Mara stayed quiet.
  - Threadline creates the question of how the fan account had the story before Jace's public clip.
  - Blink creates the question of why Jace needed the delay and who benefited.

## 9. Contradiction Ladder

- Surface belief: Mara soft-launched someone new right after Jace posted heartbreak.
- First wobble: The full Loop remix chain shows Sol and rehearsal context around the orange jacket.
- Cross-app context: Mara's Friday Frame story implies the split was already private before Sunday.
- Timeline break: The @softwatcher thread starts before Jace's public breakup clip.
- Source/method link: The fan account repeats phrasing tied to Jace's draft style and uses the cropped clip.
- Motive/benefit: Jace gains sympathy and a solo showcase slot after the public story settles.
- Confirmation bundle: Jace's Blink delay request, the Threadline timing, Sol's jacket snap, and the showcase post support WHO / WHY / WHEN / HOW together.

## 10. Report Requirements

- Correct WHO: Jace Bell shaped the public overlap story.
- Correct WHY: He wanted a sympathetic solo-breakup narrative before the showcase opportunity and did not want the queued couple content to complicate his image.
- Correct WHEN: The split happened privately Thursday; the fan thread started Sunday before Jace's public clip; the showcase benefit appeared Monday.
- Correct HOW: He used a cropped old Loop clip, fan-account amplification, and delayed public timing to make Mara look like she moved first.
- Minimum required receipts: S01-R02, S01-R04, S01-R05, S01-R07, S01-R08, S01-R10.

## 11. Plausible Wrong Theories

### Wrong Theory A: Mara cheated with Sol and hid behind styling language.

- Why plausible: The orange jacket is visually memorable, and comments treat it as romantic shorthand.
- What it explains: Why the Loop clip spread and why Mara stayed vague.
- What it fails to explain: The private Thursday breakup, the fan thread timing, and Jace's showcase benefit.
- Social-native feedback: "This reads the jacket, but it leaves the Friday story and Sunday timestamps hanging."

### Wrong Theory B: A fan account invented the whole story without Jace.

- Why plausible: @softwatcher behaves like a drama amplifier and pushes the strongest claim.
- What it explains: The source-laundered thread and viral speed.
- What it fails to explain: The thread appearing before Jace's public clip and Jace's private delay request.
- Social-native feedback: "The fan account made it loud, but it does not explain who had the timing advantage."

### Wrong Theory C: Eden was the actual reason for the breakup.

- Why plausible: The bracelet story and gossip replies create a tempting side narrative.
- What it explains: Why the relationship may have been unstable.
- What it fails to explain: The cropped studio clip, Sol's jacket, and Jace benefiting from Mara being blamed.
- Social-native feedback: "Eden explains some side-eye, not the story everyone ended up repeating."

## 12. Ending Semantics

- Public story: Mara moved on immediately and humiliated Jace.
- True story: Jace ended the relationship privately, delayed the public announcement, and helped a cropped clip make Mara look like the one who moved first.
- Emotional payoff: The player gets the satisfaction of watching a simple blame story become a constructed timeline.
- Why the false story spread: It had a clean symbol, a sad clip, a fan amplifier, and an easy person to judge.
- How the feed reacts after the report: Follow-up posts soften the Mara pile-on, @softwatcher deletes one thread, and viewers argue less about the jacket and more about Jace's timing.

## 14. Production Feasibility Notes

- Realistic photos needed: 4-6, including Mara's Frame story, the queued carousel, Sol's jacket snap, and optional Eden bracelet post.
- Short videos needed: 3 controlled vertical clips: cropped studio exit, fuller rehearsal context, and Jace's breakup clip.
- Comments/text content needed: 20-30 Loop comments, 8-12 Threadline posts/replies, 2-3 Frame captions, 2 Blink exchanges.
- Risky assets: Jace's emotional clip can feel melodramatic; the jacket continuity needs to be readable without tiny details.
- Assets that can be made from stills: Frame stories, Threadline posts, Blink messages, roster announcement, much of the remix chain.
- Assets that require careful continuity: Orange jacket, Studio 6 setting, Jace's outfit/room timing, visible timestamps.

## 15. Self-Adversarial Critique

- What is too obvious: Jace may feel too responsible once the fan thread predates his public clip.
- What is too hidden: The source-laundering link could feel weak if it rests only on phrasing; it needs timing plus benefit plus private delay.
- What app feels unnecessary: Eden's Frame side path is optional and may be cut if it distracts from Mara/Jace/Sol.
- What receipt feels too confession-like: S01-R07 is close to too strong; it must only establish the delay, not admit the public-story setup.
- Where players might get stuck: Comparing Threadline and Loop timestamps could be missed if timestamps are too small or buried.
- What feels unrealistic: A fan account posting before Jace's public clip needs a plausible reason, such as having the cropped clip first rather than mind-reading his post.
- What should change before playtesting: Make @softwatcher a recurring fan account with believable prior behavior, and keep the showcase benefit visible but not cartoonishly convenient.
