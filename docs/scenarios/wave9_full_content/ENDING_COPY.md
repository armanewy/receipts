## Ending Tone Rules

- Endings should feel like the player finally sees the feed in the right order.
- The correct ending should soften the public pile-on, not redirect it toward a new pile-on.
- Partial endings should acknowledge what the player understood without pretending the whole report is complete.
- Wrong endings should feel like a plausible feed read that missed key context.
- Keep language social-native and contained inside the fictional app world.
