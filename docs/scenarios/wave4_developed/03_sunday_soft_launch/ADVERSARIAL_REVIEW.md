# The Sunday Soft Launch - Adversarial Review

## What Still Worries The Critic

- Jace can become too obvious once players see the 9:06 PM @softwatcher thread and the 9:18 PM Jace clip.
- The source path may still feel implied rather than fully supported if @softwatcher's prior Jace connection is too faint.
- The showcase benefit can feel too neat if it looks like an instant prize instead of social momentum.
- Blink can still become too strong if Jace's Thursday message is written with too much clarity.
- The jacket symbol is strong, but it risks dominating the whole scenario if the player stops after the Sol reframe.

## What Was Improved From Wave 3

- Source linkage now has more than repeated phrasing: @softwatcher's account pattern, Jace's later public boost, and Jace's prior public access to the fuller studio context.
- Jace's private message is limited to timing and queued content. It does not explain the fan thread or his benefit.
- The benefit trail is indirect: sympathy, showcase chatter, and solo momentum rather than a direct payoff.
- Eden is clearly optional and true-but-incomplete, so the side path can be cut during playtesting.
- The report requires a cross-app bundle instead of one decisive receipt.

## What Is Likely Too Obvious

- S01-R07 plus S01-R11 may make Jace feel like the only answer before the player sees S01-R09, S01-R10, and S01-R13.
- If @softwatcher repeats Jace's phrase too exactly, it will feel like a puzzle key instead of a social pattern.
- If the showcase post appears immediately after the pile-on with celebratory language, the WHY will feel forced.

## What Is Likely Too Hidden

- Players may miss why Jace's prior access to the fuller studio context matters unless S01-R10 is surfaced naturally.
- The difference between source, amplifier, and beneficiary may be too abstract for some players.
- Timestamp comparison can be missed if app surfaces display time differently.

## What Could Be Cut

- S01-R15 Eden story should be cut if testers over-focus on a hidden-partner read.
- S01-R14 Friday casual clip can be cut if room/outfit continuity feels too visual-detail dependent.
- Some @softwatcher profile history can be trimmed if Threadline slows down the mobile rhythm.
- Duplicate jacket-context receipts can be reduced once the full content plan shows which version plays best.

## Subtlety Recommendations

- Let comments disagree in messy ways rather than pointing directly to the correct read.
- Make @softwatcher a believable fan analyst first, not a purpose-built answer account.
- Give Jace's public boost a soft social shape, such as liking support or reposting sympathy, instead of endorsing a direct claim.
- Keep Mara emotionally restrained and slightly messy, not perfectly noble.
- Present Sol's jacket context casually enough that players still need to connect it to the cropped Loop clip.

## Clarity Recommendations

- Keep the Thursday, Friday, Sunday, and Monday sequence visible in saved receipt summaries.
- Make normal app timestamps readable on mobile.
- Make the orange jacket readable in medium-sized media, not a tiny background object.
- Ensure the report screen labels HOW as the social method: crop, amplifier, delay, boost.
- Use feedback that tells the player which social context is missing without sounding procedural.

## Product Fit

The scenario fits the north star strongly. It is a browsing-first relationship truth story with a memorable visual symbol, authored payoff, all four app archetypes, and a report that emerges from comparing saved receipts.

It stays in internet drama: breakup timing, soft-launch reading, fan amplification, creator momentum, and public/private mismatch. The player remains a lurker working with social artifacts already present in the authored feed.

## Move Forward Recommendation

Move forward, with two conditions:

- During content writing, keep Jace's private message narrow and avoid turning Blink into the answer surface.
- During first-slice playtesting, watch whether players accuse Jace too early after the Threadline timestamp. If so, delay or soften S01-R09 until after the jacket reframe is complete.
