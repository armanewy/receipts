# Collab Week Is Canceled: Production Plan

| Character or account | Role | Apps | Production notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kellan Vale | Creator who shapes the public story and benefits from solo week. | Loop, Frame, Blink, Threadline mentions | Needs consistent creator persona: polished, anxious, status-aware, not openly cruel. |

## Asset Counts

- Realistic fictional photos: 5-7.
- Short videos or video-like still sequences: 2-3.
- Frame stories/highlights: 4-6.
- Threadline posts/replies/quote-posts: 14-20.
- Loop comments: 10-14.
- Blink messages/screenshots: 4-6 short artifacts.
- Profiles or bios: 3-4.
- Receipts: 14-18 possible, with 7 minimum-winning receipts.

## Realistic Photos Needed

- Kellan creator portrait or profile photo.
- Mira creator portrait or profile photo.
- Kellan solo-week teaser visual.
- Kellan casual creator setup or kitchen/desk context.
- Mira rehearsal still or behind-the-scenes still.
- Optional collab thumbnail concept.
- Optional Frame highlight cover.

## Short Videos Needed

- Kellan silent duet reaction.
- Mira rehearsal clip used in the duet.
- Optional short Loop source or audio-panel-style asset.

These can be produced as controlled short clips or still-heavy video mocks. The opening duet needs to feel socially credible, but it should not require precise acting.

## Story Screenshots Needed

- Kellan vague Frame story.
- Kellan solo-week waitlist story.
- Kellan archived solo teaser highlight.
- Mira quiet reply story.
- Optional story repost of Collab Desk recap.

## Text Posts Needed

- Collab Desk early teaser.
- Collab Desk cropped screenshot post.
- Collab Desk profile or history posts.
- Quote-posts that create public interpretation and mild phrase pattern.
- Replies linking the solo waitlist.
- Replies debating whether the screenshot is current.

## Comments Needed

- Loop opening comments: 10-14.
- Threadline replies: 12-18.
- Frame reactions/comments: 4-6 if the prototype supports them.

Comment voice should vary by confidence level: loyal Kellan fans, Mira defenders, drama lurkers, context-checkers, and tired mutual-staging skeptics.

## DMs / Snaps Needed

- Cropped Blink screenshot.
- Chat header showing older planning date.
- Mira line with surrounding launch-angle context.
- Kellan agreement reply.
- Mira still-posting question.

Keep Blink short. It should add private context, not become a full transcript.

## Continuity Objects

- "Kitchen version" as the revised collab concept.
- Solo-week visual motif visible in Frame assets.
- Collab-week date across Loop overlay, Threadline teaser, and Frame stories.
- The phrase family around camera-on performance, used softly across Threadline and Frame.
- Handles for Kellan, Mira, and Collab Desk.

## Visual Motifs

- Kellan: clean creator setup, ring light reflection, tidy solo-week branding, warm neutral backdrops.
- Mira: rehearsal space, looser creative process, kitchen/version notes, casual behind-the-scenes feel.
- Collab Desk: text-first community account, simple fictional avatar, recurring recap layout.
- Blink: compact private exchange with fictional UI and clear dates.

## Asset Risks

- Kellan may look responsible too early if solo-week assets are too polished or too prominent.
- Blink may feel too convenient if the old date and Kellan reply appear in one complete screenshot.
- Phrase repetition may feel written rather than social if exact language repeats too often.
- The opening duet may feel staged if Kellan's reaction is too theatrical.
- Sponsor-like context must remain fictional and generic.

## Easiest Assets

- Threadline posts and replies.
- Collab Desk profile/history.
- Frame waitlist story.
- Blink chat text.
- Loop comment clusters.

## Hardest Assets

- Opening Loop duet.
- Mira rehearsal clip.
- Consistent creator photos that avoid real-person resemblance.
- Frame solo-week assets that feel real but not too incriminating.
- Cropped Blink screenshot that is readable without solving everything alone.

## Receipts That Should Not Rely On Tiny Visual Detail

- CW-03 should show the saved audio/draft timing in readable metadata or caption context.
- CW-13 should make the crop edge visible enough to notice when saved, not hidden in pixels.
- CW-14 should make the old chat date prominent.
- CW-11 should make the pre-blowup date readable through highlight order or caption, not a tiny timestamp.

## Realistic-But-Fictional People Safety

- Use fully fictional names, handles, and visuals.
- Avoid public figures, influencer lookalikes, real brands, and real platform marks.
- Build character reference sheets before generating final images.
- Use partial faces, creator rooms, hands, props, and cropped bodies where continuity is hard.
- Log each generated asset with character, app, prompt intent, and continuity notes.
- Keep public callout energy contained inside the fictional scenario and ending.
