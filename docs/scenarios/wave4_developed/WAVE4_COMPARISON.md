## Candidate Notes

### The Empty Fifth Chair

Best overall package after development. It has the strongest receipt graph, clearest report mapping, and most balanced production plan. The public interpretation is highly plausible and the actual causal chain explains more without requiring a direct confession. Its main risk is that Mina's role and Cedar Room benefit can become too obvious if revealed too early.

### Collab Week Is Canceled

Best doomscroll candidate. The surface event is immediately social-native, and the wrong theories are unusually strong: Mira did it, the fan account inflated it, both creators staged it, or sponsor pressure caused the pivot. Its main risk is that Kellan is the opening narrator, so the report can be brute-forced if WHY, WHEN, and HOW are not receipt-gated.

### The Sunday Soft Launch

Best broad-audience hook. Relationship timing and soft-launch ambiguity are instantly understandable, and the jacket is a strong visual symbol. Its main risk is that the source/amplifier trail is subtler than the emotional jacket reframe, so later content writing must make the @softwatcher path feel like natural social context rather than a formal clue.

## Final Recommendation

`The Empty Fifth Chair` should be the leading MVP candidate. It is not the flashiest hook, but it has the best combination of receipt graph, app unlock clarity, production feasibility, report clarity, and adversarial robustness.

`Collab Week Is Canceled` should remain the second candidate if the team wants maximum doomscroll velocity and creator-culture energy.

`The Sunday Soft Launch` should remain the third candidate if the team wants the most accessible relationship-drama premise.

The comparison is strong enough to name `The Empty Fifth Chair` as the default Wave 5 selection, but it still needs human review before content writing because its success depends on making the friend group feel warm and real before the public cut lands.
