## Receipt Coverage

- [ ] All required receipt IDs are represented.

## App Unlocks

## Writing Quality

- [ ] Posts feel short and social-native.
- [ ] Comments have varied voices.
- [ ] DMs feel private and incomplete.
- [ ] No exposition dumps.
- [ ] No overly literary lines.
- [ ] No comments sound like instructions.

## Vocabulary And IP

- [ ] No real platform names.
- [ ] No real brand or venue names.
- [ ] No real people, public figures, or lookalikes.
- [ ] No restricted product-facing vocabulary.
- [ ] Fictional app names remain Loop, Frame, Threadline, and Blink.

## Social Realism

- [ ] Mina reads as socially plausible, not cartoonish.
- [ ] Rhea has agency and imperfection.
- [ ] The group silence has believable pressure.
- [ ] The public interpretation remains plausible.
- [ ] Wrong theories stay true-but-incomplete.
- [ ] The ending avoids real-world pile-on energy.

## Production Feasibility

- [ ] Critical visuals are readable on mobile.
- [ ] Required meaning does not rely on tiny background details.
- [ ] The table clip can be produced with controlled action.
- [ ] Still assets can carry most of the scenario.
- [ ] Continuity objects are tracked.
- [ ] Asset rights log fields are complete.

## Final QA Status

Before approving a content batch, mark one:

- Ready for next batch.
- Needs minor edits.
- Needs major rewrite.
- Blocks scenario continuity.
