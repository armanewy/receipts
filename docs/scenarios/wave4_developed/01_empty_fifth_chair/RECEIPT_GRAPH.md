## Minimum Winning Receipt Bundle

The report should require a bundle that covers all major slots:

- WHO: S02-R07, S02-R11
- WHY: S02-R10, S02-R12
- WHEN: S02-R02, S02-R04
- HOW: S02-R06, S02-R09, S02-R11
- CONTEXT: S02-R01

Minimum bundle: S02-R01, S02-R02, S02-R04, S02-R06, S02-R07, S02-R09, S02-R10, S02-R11, S02-R12.

## Alternate Acceptable Receipt Bundles

- Timeline substitute: S02-R05 can strengthen or substitute for S02-R04's timing if paired with S02-R02.
- Source substitute: S02-R08 can strengthen S02-R07 if players miss part of the Blink view alignment in S02-R11.
- Motive substitute: S02-R03 plus S02-R15 can strengthen S02-R10 and S02-R12 by showing the shared concept existed before the Cedar Room reveal.
- Group-silence context: S02-R13 can support WHY/CONTEXT when a player believes the whole group was equally responsible.
