# The Empty Fifth Chair: Adversarial Review

## What Still Worries The Critic

The scenario is strong, but it still has three major risks:

- Mina may become too obvious if Cedar Room appears before players fully believe the public Rhea story.
- The cropped-screenshot source logic may feel too technical if S02-R11 is presented as a UI detail rather than a bundle of social context.
- The remaining friends' silence may read as convenient unless their partial knowledge and access pressure are visible.

## What Improved From Wave 3

- Warm group-history content was added through S02-R03 so the empty chair has emotional weight.
- The group's silence now has a social reason through S02-R13: partial context, event access, and not wanting to be the next person pushed out.
- The Cedar Room benefit is positioned as later confirmation, not the first answer.
- The screenshot-source step now combines crop edge, contact order, admin placement, source-chain phrasing, and timing instead of relying on one tiny visual detail.
- Loop has a stronger role because the table clip is both emotionally misleading and timeline-critical.

## What Is Likely Too Obvious

- Mina is central from the first artifact, so players may name her early.
- Cedar Room gives a strong WHY once found.
- If @porchwatch's phrasing is too close to Mina's, the WHO slot could collapse before Blink.

Mitigation:

- Keep early Mina plausible as hurt host, not obvious strategist.
- Make Cedar Room visible as background status before it becomes meaningful.
- Vary Mina's table language so it is a pattern, not a direct fingerprint.

## What Is Likely Too Hidden

- The source-view alignment in S02-R11 could be missed.
- The difference between original dinner date and repost date could blur on mobile.
- Players may not understand why the "Supper Five" concept belongs to the group unless the old group-history content is clear.

Mitigation:

- Pair every subtle receipt with at least one text or timing support.
- Make timestamp comparisons legible in the content surface.
- Use menu cards, captions, and Rhea's DM to establish shared concept ownership.

## What Could Be Cut

- S02-R08 can be cut if Mina's language pattern feels too tidy or if Threadline becomes text-heavy.
- S02-R15 can be cut if Cedar Room timing is already clear from S02-R10 and S02-R12.
- Tessa can stay atmospheric and should not receive extra receipt-critical content unless needed.
- Duplicate group-history posts should be cut if they do not deepen the empty-chair hook.

## Should This Scenario Move Forward?

Yes. It is one of the best Wave 4 candidates because it has a strong hook, a graph-like receipt structure, meaningful use of all four app archetypes, and manageable production scope. The main next-step requirement is disciplined content writing so the social posts feel casual rather than engineered.
