# Media Generation Guide

## Safety Rules

- All people are fictional.
- Do not use public figures, influencers, or lookalikes.
- Do not use real platform logos or real app UI.
- Do not use real venue branding.
- Keep fictional handles and account names consistent.
- Maintain an asset rights log for every generated or edited asset.

## Cast Continuity

Create fictional cast reference sheets before final media generation:

- Mina: warm table-host styling, polished casual outfits, confident posture.
- Rhea: creative contributor styling, practical clothes, visible notebook/menu props.
- Dax: casual social videographer energy.
- Nia, Sol, Tessa: supporting group presence; avoid over-designing them.

Do not base references on real people.

## Continuity Objects

Use recurring objects instead of tiny facial details where possible:

- Empty fifth chair.
- Rhea place card or gift bag.
- Handwritten menu cards.
- Supper Five planning notebook.
- Cedar Room poster.
- Shared dishes, candles, table linens.
- Birthday table layout.

## Per-App Visual Style

### Frame

- Polished but casual photo/story composition.
- Warm tones for group history.
- Cooler or cleaner framing for empty-chair story.
- Stories should feel familiar without copying exact real app UI.

### Loop

- Vertical short-video composition.
- Quick caption overlays.
- Comment-heavy surface.
- Use controlled movement or still-based video if acting quality is a risk.

### Threadline

- Text-first screenshots.
- Short posts, quote reactions, visible timestamps.
- Fictional UI with no real-platform branding.

### Blink

- Private message screenshots.
- Minimal, readable timestamps.
- Cropped/uncropped chat continuity must be clear.
- Avoid long chat walls.

## Photo Guidance

- Prefer partial faces, hands, table settings, notebooks, menus, and group compositions when continuity is hard.
- Do not make required meaning depend on tiny background text.
- Use story captions and comments to reinforce visual meaning.
- Generate multiple variations of the empty-chair composition and test mobile readability.

## Video Guidance

- Keep video receipts short and controlled.
- The table clip should show Rhea leaving, not explain why.
- Caption/repost context should carry the public interpretation.
- Avoid complex choreography.
- A still-frame sequence may be acceptable for early prototype.

## Asset Rights Log Fields

- Asset ID.
- Receipt ID, if any.
- App surface.
- Generation date.
- Prompt summary.
- Model/tool used.
- Human edits made.
- Fictional cast references used.
- Real-person avoidance check.
- Brand/platform avoidance check.
- Continuity notes.
- Approval status.
