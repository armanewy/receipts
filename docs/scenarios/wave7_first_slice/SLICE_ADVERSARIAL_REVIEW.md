# Slice Adversarial Review

## Is The First 30 Seconds Strong?

Yes, if the empty chair image is visually clear and the caption is sharp without feeling over-written. The hook is readable: a friend has been publicly cut out, and screenshots are the implied reason.

## Is The Content Too Obvious?

Moderate risk. Mina owns the first story, the highlight rename, and the pinned reply. If her language is too pointed, players may identify her as the answer before Loop.

Mitigation:

- Keep Mina emotionally plausible.
- Let the comments support the public Rhea reading.
- Do not foreground Cedar Room in the first slice.

## Is The Content Too Obscure?

Moderate risk around S02-R02. The highlight rename timing may be too subtle unless the UI makes the recent rename readable.

Mitigation:

- Make the old thumbnail say `Supper Five Planning Night`.
- Make the current highlight say `Supper Four`.
- Use a clear "updated Wednesday morning" style surface in the prototype.

## Does It Feel Like Fake Social Browsing?

It can work if comments vary in tone. The current comment set includes support, gossip, confusion, and casual phrasing. It still needs playtest tuning so the Dax/Loop references do not feel too coordinated.

## Does It Create A Reason To Continue?

Yes. The player should want to open Loop because several comments and the repost sticker imply the table clip is the emotional version of the story.

## Is The First Unlock Organic?

Yes. A repost sticker plus social comments is a natural Frame-to-Loop path. The risk is overuse of comments as app directions, so future slices should vary unlock triggers.

## What Should Change Before Creating The Rest?

- Test two versions of Mina's caption: one sharper, one softer.
- Keep Cedar Room nearly invisible in the first slice.
- Add one warmer Rhea/Mina old-history moment if testers do not care about the group.
- Reduce Dax comments if the Loop unlock feels too pushed.
- Make S02-R02's timing visible without making it feel like a formal timestamp puzzle.
