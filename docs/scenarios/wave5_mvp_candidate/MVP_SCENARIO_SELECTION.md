# MVP Scenario Selection

## Chosen Scenario

The Empty Fifth Chair.

Source packet: `docs/scenarios/wave4_developed/01_empty_fifth_chair/`.

## Why It Won

The Empty Fifth Chair is the strongest MVP candidate because it has the best balance of first-screen clarity, receipt graph depth, app unlock logic, and production feasibility.

It won on:

- Strong visual hook: the empty fifth chair is immediately readable on mobile.
- Best receipt graph: 15 receipts with required, optional, misleading, and substitute roles.
- Clearest cross-app path: Frame to Loop to Threadline to Blink, then back to Frame and Threadline.
- Strong report mapping: WHO, WHY, WHEN, and HOW each require different receipt types.
- Feasible production: mostly stills, text, comments, story screenshots, and one controlled clip.
- Individual responsible party: Mina Saye shapes the public story.
- Non-crime internet drama: friend-group fallout, shared credit, status pressure, and public curation.
- Strong wrong theories: Rhea, @porchwatch, Dax, the whole group, and Cedar Room each explain partial context.

## What Was Rejected And Why

### Collab Week Is Canceled

Rejected for MVP selection, not discarded. It has the strongest doomscroll momentum and excellent creator-drama energy, but it requires more careful creator-video execution, more fan-account texture, and stricter reveal pacing so Kellan does not become too obvious too early.

### The Sunday Soft Launch

Rejected for MVP selection, not discarded. It has the broadest relationship-drama hook, but its source/amplifier/benefit path is subtler and depends on precise writing around @softwatcher, the jacket reframe, and Jace's indirect benefit.

## What Needs Human Review

- Whether the empty-chair hook feels fresh enough compared with other social exclusion stories.
- Whether the supper-club / Cedar Room status world is compelling to the target audience.
- Whether Mina's motive feels strong enough without making her too obvious.
- Whether Rhea has enough personality and agency before being publicly framed.
- Whether the friend group should be older creator-world adults, college-age creators, or a more general community circle.
- Whether the final report should use "report" or a more social-native surface name in UI.

## What Remains Uncertain

- How much warm group-history content is needed before the empty chair lands emotionally.
- How subtle the Mina-to-@porchwatch source path can be before players miss it.
- Whether the Cedar Room announcement should appear midway or late in the first playable build.
- Whether the MVP should include the optional Nia/Sol side DM in the first full prototype.
- How many atmospheric posts are needed to avoid the feed feeling overly constructed.

## Ready For Human Review

Yes. The scenario is coherent enough for human review and content-blueprint planning. It should not proceed to full content writing until the group-world tone and Mina/Rhea relationship texture are approved.
