## Beat Pacing Notes

- Beats 1-4 should fit the first 5-8 minutes and deliver the first wobble plus first app unlock.
- Beats 5-10 carry the main doomscroll pull: each answer creates a sharper reason to tap another source.
- Beats 11-14 should feel like confirmation through accumulation, not one private message solving everything.
- Beats 15-16 are optional reinforcement and report assembly.

## App Texture Goals

- Loop should feel fast, visual, and emotionally reactive.
- Frame should feel curated, ambiguous, and socially close.
- Threadline should feel public, messy, and timestamp-rich.
- Blink should feel brief, cropped, and contextual, not omniscient.

## Micro-Reward Pattern

The player repeatedly gets a small payoff:

- A symbol gains context.
- A timestamp flips the order.
- A public account becomes less random.
- A private message explains silence.
- A benefit trail makes the public posture feel purposeful.

Each payoff should make the player want one more receipt rather than sending them straight to the report.
