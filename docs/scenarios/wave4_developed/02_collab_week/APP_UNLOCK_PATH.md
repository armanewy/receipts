# Collab Week Is Canceled: App Unlock Path

## Starting App

Loop starts unlocked because the scenario needs an immediate creator-drama hook. The opening duet gives the player the public version before any context work: Kellan looks hurt, Mira looks careless, and collab week looks freshly canceled.

## Unlock Sequence Overview

| Stage | App unlocked | Semantic trigger | What the player likely knows | Question motivating the tap | What the new app reveals |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Start | Loop | Viral duet appears in the feed. | Kellan is publicly reacting to Mira, and commenters mention a private screenshot. | What did Mira actually say? | Emotional hook, public blame, and first route to the screenshot. |
| Unlock 1 | Threadline | Loop comments tag Collab Desk and say the full recap is there. | The screenshot is shaping the public story. | Who posted the screenshot first, and how did it spread? | Early teaser timing, cropped quote, and recap-account amplification. |
| Unlock 2 | Frame | Threadline replies repost Kellan's Frame story and his Loop profile links the same Frame handle. | The recap account may have framed the story before the duet peaked. | What was Kellan doing while the story spread? | Solo-week waitlist, vague story, and prebuilt rollout context. |
| Unlock 3 | Blink | The cropped Threadline screenshot includes a Blink label, and Mira's Frame reply points to an old planning chat. | The quote may be old or cropped. | What did the private exchange actually mean? | Older chat context, the surrounding launch-angle discussion, and Kellan's agreement. |

## Exact Semantic Trigger For Each New App

### Loop To Threadline

The trigger is not a tutorial prompt. It is social behavior: multiple comments reference Collab Desk as the place where people are seeing the screenshot. One comment says the recap posted before Kellan's duet reached the feed. That makes Threadline feel like the natural next tap.

In-world phrasing examples:

- "Collab Desk has the screen grab."
- "Why did their recap post go up before this blew up?"
- "Threadline is already on fire over this."

### Threadline To Frame

The trigger is comparison pressure. Collab Desk references Kellan's camera-on phrasing, and replies repost a Frame story where Kellan uses similar language. The player does not unlock Frame because a game tells them to; the player wants to see whether Kellan's public story matches the recap's framing.

In-world phrasing examples:

- "He posted a story too, same vibe."
- "His Frame link is in bio if you missed the waitlist thing."
- "This is turning into solo-week promo already."

### Frame To Blink

The trigger is the crop's missing context. Mira's quiet Frame reply says the line is old planning context, while the Threadline screenshot still shows a Blink label and an edge of Kellan's next bubble. Blink becomes available as surfaced saved context, not as invasive access.

In-world phrasing examples:

- "The old launch chat is circulating now."
- "That crop stops right before his reply."
- "Mira says it was from the kitchen-version planning chat."

## What The Player Has Likely Learned Before Each Unlock

Before Threadline:

- The duet frames Mira as the person who damaged collab week.
- Fans are treating a Blink screenshot as the key artifact.
- The story is already emotionally charged.

Before Frame:

- The recap account posted early enough to create timing friction.
- The cropped quote is doing most of the blame work.
- The recap account may be an amplifier rather than the origin of the framing.

Before Blink:

- Kellan's solo rollout was not invented after the blowup.
- Frame and Threadline use similar social framing.
- Mira is pushing back by saying the quote is old, but she has less audience momentum.

## What Question Each Unlock Answers

- Threadline answers: how did the screenshot spread?
- Frame answers: who benefited from the story becoming simple and public?
- Blink answers: what did the cropped quote mean before it was reposted?

## What New Question Each Unlock Creates

- Loop creates: where is the screenshot, and is Mira really mocking him?
- Threadline creates: why did the recap account move before the public trigger?
- Frame creates: why was the solo path already ready?
- Blink creates: why was Kellan's agreement hidden from the public version?

## How To Avoid Tutorial-ish Language

- Use comments, repost captions, profile links, visible handles, story reposts, and screenshot labels as the unlock language.
- Avoid explicit prompts like "go to Threadline" or "new app unlocked."
- Let the app tray change in response to saved receipts and tapped handles, while the visible text remains social-native.
- Use tiny in-world labels such as "people are reposting this on Threadline" rather than mechanical objective text.

## App Availability Phrasing

Good phrasing:

- "More from Collab Desk"
- "Kellan's Frame story"
- "Saved Blink thread"
- "Mira's old planning chat"
- "Quote-posts around the crop"

Avoid phrasing that sounds like a puzzle step or formal task. The player should feel like they are following social context.
