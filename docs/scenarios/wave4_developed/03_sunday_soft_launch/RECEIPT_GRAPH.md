## Minimum Winning Receipt Bundle

The leanest correct bundle should require the player to connect different kinds of receipts:

- S01-R02: the cropped jacket read lacks full rehearsal context.
- S01-R04: Mara's public vagueness predates the Sunday pile-on.
- S01-R07: @softwatcher's thread starts before Jace's public breakup clip.
- S01-R09: Jace boosts the cropped interpretation after it begins spreading.
- S01-R10: Jace had public access to the fuller studio context.
- S01-R11: Jace requested delayed public clarity on Thursday.
- S01-R13: Jace benefits from sympathy and solo showcase momentum.

This bundle supports WHO, WHY, WHEN, and HOW without letting one private receipt carry the whole answer.

## Alternate Acceptable Receipt Bundles

- Jacket-method bundle: S01-R02 + S01-R06 + S01-R12 can replace one another as jacket-context support, but at least two should be saved for strong confidence.
- Timing bundle: S01-R03 + S01-R07 + S01-R11 can establish that the public story order is inverted.
- Source-path bundle: S01-R07 + S01-R08 + S01-R09 + S01-R10 can establish that @softwatcher was not random noise and that Jace helped the story travel.
- Benefit bundle: S01-R05 + S01-R13 can establish why delayed public timing mattered, with S01-R11 linking that reason to Jace.

## Receipts That Are Only Red Herrings

- S01-R01 is the starting misleading receipt. It is true as an artifact but wrong as a relationship read.
- S01-R15 is a true-but-incomplete gossip path. It supports the idea that people are over-reading symbols but should not be required.

## Receipts That Reinforce But Are Not Required

- S01-R03 reinforces the public clip timing but should be paired with S01-R07.
- S01-R08 adds texture to @softwatcher but is not enough by itself.
- S01-R14 strengthens the prepared-clip read but should not become a required pixel-comparison task.

## Design Notes

- No single receipt should state that Jace created the whole pile-on.
- Timestamps should be ordinary visible app context, not hidden metadata.
- Critical support should come from the bundle: private delay, public source timing, jacket reframe, Jace's public access to context, and benefit trail.
- Eden should remain optional and easy to cut if playtesters over-focus on that side path.
