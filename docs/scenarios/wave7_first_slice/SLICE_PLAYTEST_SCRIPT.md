# Slice Playtest Script

## Purpose

Test whether the first Frame slice creates curiosity, initial judgment, first doubt, and a natural desire to open Loop.

## What The Tester Sees

1. Mina's Frame story carousel.
2. Empty fifth chair and screenshot caption.
3. Story comments/reactions.
4. Mina profile with `Supper Four` highlight.
5. Old `Supper Five` highlight card showing Rhea's contribution.
6. Repost sticker to Dax's Loop table clip.

## What The Tester Can Tap

- Save S02-R01.
- Tap Mina's profile.
- Tap `Supper Four`.
- Tap old highlight thumbnail.
- Open Rhea's profile snippet.
- Read story comments.
- Tap the Loop repost sticker.

## What The Tester Should Understand

By the end:

- Rhea is the missing friend.
- Mina's group appears to believe Rhea leaked something private.
- The group used to include five people.
- Rhea had some real connection to the supper concept.
- The table clip is the next emotionally important surface.
- The highlight rename timing feels slightly too early or too neat.

## What The Tester Might Misunderstand

- They may think Rhea is already proven responsible.
- They may think Mina is obviously responsible too early.
- They may miss the timing meaning of `Supper Four`.
- They may over-focus on Cedar Room if the profile teaser is too visible.
- They may not care about Rhea unless old group warmth appears early enough.

## Questions To Ask After The Slice

1. What do you think happened between Mina and Rhea?
2. Which post or story made you want to tap deeper?
3. Did the empty chair feel like a real social post?
4. Did you notice anything that did not fit?
5. Would you open the Loop clip? Why or why not?
6. Did any comment feel like it was written to guide you?
7. Who feels responsible right now, if anyone?
8. What receipt would you save first?

## Pass Criteria

- Tester wants to open Loop.
- Tester can summarize the public interpretation.
- Tester notices either the `Supper Four` rename or the old `Supper Five` warmth.
- Tester does not fully solve Mina's role.
- Tester does not feel like the content is tutorialized.

## Fail Criteria

- Tester has no reason to care about Rhea.
- Tester thinks Mina is clearly the answer already.
- Tester misses the Loop unlock entirely.
- Tester says comments feel like hints.
- Tester cannot tell what the public story is.
- Tester thinks the story is about a venue or organization rather than the friend group.
