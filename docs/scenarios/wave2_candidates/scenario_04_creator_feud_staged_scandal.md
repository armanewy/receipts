# Scenario 04: Collab Week Is Canceled

## 1. Scenario Metadata

- Title: Collab Week Is Canceled
- Category: Creator feud / staged scandal
- Tone: public embarrassment, opportunistic creator drama, fast-moving feed energy
- Estimated playtime: 25-35 minutes
- Intended difficulty: Medium
- Starting app: Loop
- Apps used: Loop, Frame, Threadline, Blink
- Primary transformation types: temporal inversion, selective cropping, source laundering, incentive trail, public/private mismatch, old-content reframing, amplifier/source separation
- One-sentence semantic promise: A creator appears to have been betrayed by his collab partner, but the receipts show he staged the feud himself to escape a weak collab and redirect attention to a solo launch.

## 2. Surface Event Semantics

- Artifact type: Loop duet where Kellan Vale reacts silently to Mira Sol's old rehearsal clip while overlay text says "collab week was supposed to start today."
- Emotional charge: betrayal, secondhand embarrassment, team-picking, creator fandom loyalty.
- Apparent target: Mira Sol, the collab partner framed as careless and disloyal.
- Immediate question: Did Mira leak, mock, or sabotage the collab right before launch?
- Why a user keeps scrolling: The duet is ambiguous, the comments are split, a fan account claims to have "the Blink screenshot," and Kellan's Frame story says "some people only show up when cameras are on."

## 3. Public Interpretation Semantics

- Apparent actor: Mira Sol.
- Apparent motive: She wanted to distance herself from Kellan after realizing his channel was losing momentum.
- Apparent timeline: Mira mocked the collab privately, a screenshot leaked, Kellan saw it, and he canceled collab week in response.
- Apparent wronged party: Kellan Vale.
- Viral supporting artifact: A cropped Blink screenshot where Mira says "this collab is going to look desperate" under Kellan's name.
- Emotional reason it spreads: It gives fans a clean betrayal story and lets them defend the creator they already like.
- What it gets right: Mira did say the line, the collab was shaky, and Kellan was anxious about launch numbers.
- What it gets wrong: The screenshot was from an older planning exchange, Kellan cropped the surrounding context, and he seeded the fan-account leak before Mira posted anything public.

## 4. Actual Causal Chain

- Responsible individual: Kellan Vale.
- Motive/pressure: He had a sponsor-adjacent solo launch ready and feared the joint collab would make him look dependent on Mira.
- Action taken: He revived an old Blink message, cropped out his own agreement, fed it to a fan recap account, and posted a Loop duet that made the leak feel like a response rather than a setup.
- Manipulation/concealment method: Old-content reframing, selective cropping, source laundering through a fan account, and temporal inversion of cause and reaction.
- True timeline: Kellan sent the cropped screenshot to the recap account before Mira's rehearsal clip resurfaced; his "hurt" Loop duet was posted after the fan account already queued the thread.
- Intended public belief: Mira betrayed the collab and forced Kellan into canceling it.
- Unintended trace left behind: The fan thread timestamp precedes the public trigger, Kellan's Frame media kit shows the solo launch already scheduled, and a Blink crop edge leaves part of his own reply bubble visible.
- Why this explanation is more satisfying: It explains why the leak moved so neatly, why Kellan's solo announcement was ready, why Mira's statement sounded confused, and why the supposedly fresh screenshot contained old planning language.

## 5. Transformation Types Used

- Temporal inversion: Kellan's Loop duet is framed as a reaction, but the leak machine started before the public clip.
- Selective cropping: The Blink screenshot hides Kellan agreeing that the first collab pitch looked desperate.
- Source laundering: A fan recap account makes Kellan's planted screenshot appear independent.
- Incentive trail: Kellan's solo launch, sponsor-facing deck, and affiliate link all benefit from sympathy traffic.
- Public/private mismatch: Public heartbreak contrasts with private planning where both creators knew the collab needed changes.
- Old-content reframing: A weeks-old planning note becomes a fresh insult.
- Amplifier/source separation: The loud fan account is not the true source of the screenshot.

## 6. App Semantic Roles

### Loop

- Role in scenario: Creates the emotional hook and spreads the apparent betrayal.
- Key artifact: Kellan's silent duet with Mira's rehearsal clip and launch-day overlay.
- Misleading surface: Kellan looks like he is reacting in real time to Mira's disrespect.
- Truth it reveals: The duet audio and upload sequence show it was prepared before the thread peaked.
- Unlock trigger: Comments ask why the "first source" is on Threadline.
- Adversarial concern: The staged reaction should feel like real creator behavior, not theatrical villainy.

### Frame

- Role in scenario: Shows brand timing, social performance, and media-kit readiness.
- Key artifact: Kellan's story about "showing up when cameras are on" beside a pre-scheduled solo launch teaser.
- Misleading surface: The story reads like hurt, vague-posted disappointment.
- Truth it reveals: Kellan had solo rollout assets ready before the alleged betrayal.
- Unlock trigger: A story sticker references the fan recap thread and a saved Blink screenshot.
- Adversarial concern: Sponsor-like context must stay fictional and not become a company-wide plot.

### Threadline

- Role in scenario: Maps how the story traveled from source to amplifier.
- Key artifact: Fan recap thread posting the cropped Blink screenshot and a quote chain blaming Mira.
- Misleading surface: The recap account appears to have received the screenshot from a neutral insider.
- Truth it reveals: The first fan post went live before Mira's rehearsal clip was supposedly the trigger, and the wording mirrors Kellan's Frame captions.
- Unlock trigger: The screenshot includes a Blink contact label that leads to the private exchange.
- Adversarial concern: Too many public posts can bog down the flow; keep the chain finite.

### Blink

- Role in scenario: Reframes the cropped quote and shows the old planning context.
- Key artifact: Longer Blink exchange where Mira's "desperate" line refers to a rejected launch angle, followed by Kellan agreeing.
- Misleading surface: The cropped line sounds cruel out of context.
- Truth it reveals: The quote was old, mutual, and not a betrayal; Kellan chose the crop.
- Unlock trigger: Threadline's screenshot exposes enough of the contact label and timestamp to open the message path.
- Adversarial concern: The private exchange cannot be a single total answer; it should work with timing and motive receipts.

## 7. Progressive App Disclosure

- Starting app and why: Loop opens with a viral duet that is emotionally readable without background knowledge.
- Unlock 2 trigger: Loop comments name the Threadline recap account as the source of the screenshot.
- Unlock 3 trigger: Threadline's screenshot and replies point to Kellan's Frame story and media-kit teaser.
- Unlock 4 trigger: Frame story sticker and Threadline screenshot reveal a Blink contact label tied to the old planning chat.
- What question each unlock answers: Threadline answers where the screenshot spread; Frame answers who benefited; Blink answers what the quote meant.
- What new question each unlock creates: Threadline raises timing problems; Frame raises motive; Blink raises why Kellan cropped his own agreement.

## 9. Contradiction Ladder

- Surface belief: Mira privately mocked Kellan and ruined collab week.
- First wobble: The recap thread teases the drama before the Loop duet becomes the public trigger.
- Cross-app context: Frame shows Kellan's solo launch assets were ready before the blowup.
- Timeline break: Blink shows the quoted line was three weeks old and from a shared planning chat.
- Source/method link: The recap account repeats Kellan's exact framing before his story goes up.
- Motive/benefit: Kellan redirects attention to a solo launch and avoids a shaky collab.
- Confirmation bundle: R04-03, R04-04, R04-05, R04-06, R04-09, and R04-10 support the report without relying on one receipt.

## 10. Report Requirements

- Correct WHO: Kellan Vale.
- Correct WHY: He wanted sympathy traffic and a clean pivot into his solo launch instead of sharing a weak collab week with Mira.
- Correct WHEN: He seeded the cropped screenshot before the public Loop blowup and used a three-week-old message as if it were fresh.
- Correct HOW: He cropped an old Blink planning exchange, fed it through a fan recap account, then posted a prepared Loop duet and Frame vague-posts to guide interpretation.
- Minimum required receipts: R04-03, R04-04, R04-05, R04-06, R04-09, R04-10.
- Optional supporting receipts: R04-02, R04-07, R04-08, R04-11, R04-12.
- Accepted substitute receipt bundles: R04-04 plus R04-11 can support early preparation; R04-05 plus R04-06 plus R04-07 can support motive/benefit if one Frame receipt is missed.

## 11. Plausible Wrong Theories

### Mira sabotaged collab week

- Why plausible: Her cropped quote sounds harsh and Kellan's reaction looks hurt.
- What it explains: The public outrage and why fans defend Kellan.
- What it fails to explain: The old timestamp, Kellan's prebuilt solo assets, and the recap account's early framing.
- Social-native feedback: "This fits the clipped quote, but not why the rollout was ready before the blowup."

### The fan recap account invented the feud for engagement

- Why plausible: The account benefits from drama and posts first.
- What it explains: Source laundering and fast spread.
- What it fails to explain: The account's access to the crop and its use of Kellan's phrasing.
- Social-native feedback: "The recap account made it louder, but the framing came from closer to Kellan."

### Both creators staged it together

- Why plausible: Creator drama can be mutual promotion, and both gained attention.
- What it explains: The polished timing and the public spike.
- What it fails to explain: Mira's confused follow-up and the crop hiding Kellan's own agreement.
- Social-native feedback: "The attention helped both feeds, but the crop only protects one person."

## 12. Ending Semantics

- Public story: Mira betrayed Kellan right before collab week, so he had to cancel and move on.
- True story: Kellan staged the public feud by cropping old context and laundering it through a fan recap account to make his solo pivot look justified.
- Emotional payoff: The player sees a messy public breakup of a creator partnership become a calculated attention redirect.
- Why the false story spread: It used a short cruel-looking quote, a hurt reaction clip, and a fan account that seemed independent.
- How the feed reacts after the report: Some fans call out the crop, some argue the collab was doomed anyway, and Mira's quieter posts read less evasive in hindsight.

## 14. Production Feasibility Notes

- Realistic photos needed: 3-5 Frame story/carousel images for Kellan's solo rollout and creator lifestyle context.
- Short videos needed: 2-3 Loop clips: Kellan duet, Mira rehearsal clip, optional reaction clip.
- Comments/text content needed: 25-35 public comments/posts and 2-3 Blink snippets.
- Risky assets: Cropped Blink screenshot must be readable but not feel like an instant complete answer; sponsor-like materials must be fictional.
- Assets that can be made from stills: Loop duet, Frame stories, Threadline thread screenshots, media-kit carousel.
- Assets that require careful continuity: Dates across Threadline, Frame, Blink, and Loop; exact wording around "cameras are on"; old Blink crop.

## 15. Self-Adversarial Critique

- What is too obvious: The solo launch assets may make Kellan look responsible too early if presented before the timing contradiction.
- What is too hidden: The recap account repeating Kellan's phrasing could be missed unless the phrase is memorable but natural.
- What app feels unnecessary: None, though Frame's motive receipts must do more than show Kellan vague-posting.
- What receipt feels too confession-like: The expanded Blink crop could solve too much if it includes an overly neat Kellan reply; keep it about shared planning context.
- Where players might get stuck: They may assume "both creators staged it" because creator feuds often look manufactured.
- What feels unrealistic: A creator might not expose his own staged setup through media-kit timing unless the assets are public but overlooked.
- What should be changed before playtesting: Make Mira's public confusion clear enough to support doubt, and ensure Kellan's benefit is visible without making him cartoonish.
