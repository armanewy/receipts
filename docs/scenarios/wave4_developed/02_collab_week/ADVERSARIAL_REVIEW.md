## What Still Worries The Critic

- Kellan is the opening narrator of the surface story, so some players may guess him early.
- The scenario uses common transformation types: cropping, old content, and timing. It needs strong execution to avoid feeling familiar.
- The solo-week benefit can become too obvious if Frame is encountered before Threadline timing has created doubt.
- Blink is powerful. If the private context is too neat, it could flatten the report into one app's answer.
- The ending tone must avoid encouraging a pile-on against Kellan. The payoff should be about how the story was shaped, not punishing him.

Yes. It has the strongest doomscroll energy among the top candidates and a clear cross-app path. It should move forward if the team wants an MVP scenario with immediate public tension, manageable asset production, and strong wrong theories.
