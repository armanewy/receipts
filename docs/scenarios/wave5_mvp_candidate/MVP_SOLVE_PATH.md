# MVP Solve Path

## Starting App

Frame.

The player starts with Mina's public story carousel because Frame is the cleanest surface for the social cut: an empty chair, old highlight names, tagged friends, and group-history thumbnails.

## First Surface Artifact

S02-R01: Mina's birthday dinner story shows four friends, one empty fifth chair, and a pointed caption about screenshots.

What the player should think first: Rhea is missing because she broke trust.

## First Receipt

S02-R01 can be saved immediately. It is required as context, but it does not prove WHO, WHY, WHEN, or HOW alone.

## First Contradiction

S02-R02: Mina's highlight changed from "Supper Five" to "Supper Four" before the @porchwatch crop became the main public artifact.

The player should wonder: if the group cut was a response to the screenshot, why did the public curation shift first?

## App Unlock 1: Loop

Frame unlocks Loop through Mina's repost sticker and comments naming Dax's table clip.

Receipts found there:

- S02-R04: Dax's table clip of Rhea leaving during Mina's toast.
- S02-R05: Comment friction saying the clip was from Tuesday.

The public story gets stronger for a moment because the clip makes Rhea look guilty or dramatic. Then the date correction creates the first timing wobble.

## App Unlock 2: Threadline

Loop unlocks Threadline through comments naming @porchwatch's screenshot.

Receipts found there:

- S02-R06: Cropped Blink screenshot.
- S02-R07: @porchwatch source-chain post.
- S02-R08: Mina's older table-language post.

The player should now wonder who had the crop and why the screenshot traveled through a small account rather than directly from the group.

## App Unlock 3: Blink

Threadline unlocks Blink through the exposed "Supper Five Planning" group-chat name and replies pointing out that the crop begins mid-conversation.

Receipts found there:

- S02-R09: Uncropped group chat.
- S02-R10: Rhea/Mina credit DM.
- S02-R11: Crop-source alignment bundle.
- S02-R13: Nia/Sol side DM about partial context and access pressure.

Blink changes the meaning of the public screenshot. Rhea was trying to slow the private conflict down, Mina's removed lines mattered, and Cedar Room becomes the next question.

## Return Path: Frame And Threadline

Blink sends the player back to Frame because Rhea's DM mentions Thursday and Cedar Room.

Receipts found on return:

- S02-R12: Cedar Room solo host announcement.
- S02-R15: Mina profile update before the public pile-on.
- S02-R14: @porchwatch locked-replies update after timing/source questions spread.

## When The Public Theory Collapses

The public theory collapses when the player has:

- S02-R02 showing the group image changed early.
- S02-R04 or S02-R05 showing the Loop clip was out of order.
- S02-R09 showing the cropped screenshot removed meaning-changing context.

At that point, Rhea as direct leaker no longer explains the timeline or the chat content.

## When The True Theory Becomes Likely

The true theory becomes likely when S02-R10 connects the private conflict to Cedar Room and S02-R12 shows Mina's solo benefit.

The player can now read Mina not only as a hurt host, but as someone with a reason to shift the group story before Thursday.

## When The True Theory Becomes Report-Ready

The true theory becomes report-ready when the player has S02-R11 plus at least one Threadline source-chain receipt.

S02-R11 should not act alone. It becomes strong when paired with:

- S02-R07 for source-chain direction.
- S02-R09 for missing chat context.
- S02-R02 and S02-R04 for public timing.
- S02-R10 and S02-R12 for benefit.

## Report Assembly

Correct assembly:

- WHO: Mina Saye.
- WHY: Cedar Room pressure and shared-concept credit.
- WHEN: Frame curation before the public crop spread; Tuesday clip framed as Wednesday reaction.
- HOW: Cropped Blink chat, @porchwatch amplification, Frame exclusion image, and out-of-order Loop clip.
- RECEIPTS: S02-R01, S02-R02, S02-R04, S02-R06, S02-R07, S02-R09, S02-R10, S02-R11, S02-R12.

## Ending Payoff

The ending should reframe the feed rather than punish anyone. The best payoff is seeing the saved receipts change meaning:

- The empty chair was staged as a response, but it came early.
- The table clip was emotional, but out of order.
- The screenshot was real, but cropped.
- The venue announcement was not the whole story, but it explains the pressure.
- The group silence was uncomfortable and partial, not proof that the public version was right.
