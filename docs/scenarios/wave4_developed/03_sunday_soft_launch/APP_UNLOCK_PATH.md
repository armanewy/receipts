# The Sunday Soft Launch - App Unlock Path

## Starting App And Why

Loop starts unlocked because the strongest hook is a cropped vertical clip with immediate emotional readability. The player can understand the public story in seconds: Mara in an orange jacket, Jace's heartbreak nearby, comments treating the timing as cruel.

Loop should feel like an open feed, not an entry screen. The player scrolls into the drama, saves the cropped clip, and follows familiar social references.

## Unlock 2: Frame

### Exact Semantic Trigger

Loop comments and profile captions repeatedly reference Mara's Friday Frame story and the queued couple carousel. The trigger is not a tutorial prompt; it is social curiosity:

- Viewers argue about whether Mara had already hinted that the breakup timing was out of her hands.
- A comment mentions the Friday story and says the Frame post makes the Sunday clip feel off.
- Mara's Loop profile has a visible Frame handle.

### What The Player Has Likely Learned Before Unlock

- The feed thinks Mara moved on too fast.
- The jacket is the visual symbol.
- Jace's breakup clip and Mara's studio clip are being paired by comments.

### Question Motivating The Unlock

Did Mara's own posts support the public timeline, or was she already signaling a delayed announcement?

### What Frame Reveals

Frame reveals Mara's Friday "not mine to post yet" story, the queued couple carousel, and Sol's studio styling context. It turns Mara's vagueness from image management into a timing question.

### New Question Created

If Mara was waiting because public timing was not hers to control, who wanted the delay and why did the cropped jacket story become loud on Sunday?

## How To Avoid Tutorial-ish Language

- Use in-world references: handles, tags, quoted posts, story previews, and comments.
- Avoid commands like "unlock Frame now" or "go to Threadline for more."
- Use social-native availability language such as "Mara's Frame is getting tagged in this" or "People keep quoting @softwatcher."
- Let the player tap the app surface because a post makes them curious, not because the system announces the next step.

## In-World Availability Phrasing

- Frame: "Mara's Friday story is all over the replies."
- Threadline: "@softwatcher started the jacket thread before Jace posted?"
- Blink: "Someone saved the Thursday screenshots, but the crop is messy."

## Unlock Quality Checks

- Each unlock answers one question and creates one sharper question.
- No app opens only to provide a report slot.
- Blink arrives after public surfaces have already created tension, so it does not feel like a late answer dump.
- Threadline source path should feel browsed through posts and replies, not summarized by a narrator.
