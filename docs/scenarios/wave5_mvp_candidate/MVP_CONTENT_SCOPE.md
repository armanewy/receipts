# MVP Content Scope

## App Screens

Estimated MVP screens:

- Frame: 8-10 screens.
- Loop: 4-5 screens.
- Threadline: 6-8 screens.
- Blink: 5-6 screens.
- Report: 4-6 screens or states.
- Total: roughly 27-35 authored screens/states.

## Posts Per App

| App | Core posts/artifacts | Atmospheric posts/artifacts |
|---|---:|---:|
| Frame | 5 | 4-6 |
| Loop | 2 | 2-3 |
| Threadline | 4 | 4-6 |
| Blink | 4 | 1-2 |

## Comments

Target count:

- Frame comments/reactions: 8-12.
- Loop comments: 12-18.
- Threadline replies/quote-posts: 15-20.
- Total comments/replies: 35-50.

Comments must feel varied and social-native. They should not read like instructions.
