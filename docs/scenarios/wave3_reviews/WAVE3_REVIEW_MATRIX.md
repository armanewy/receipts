## Recurring Systemic Problems

- Cropped screenshots and clips are doing a lot of work across the set; future revisions should vary transformation types more aggressively.
- Timing contradictions are useful but risk becoming puzzle-like if timestamps become the primary play pattern.
- Several scenarios make the responsible individual visible early enough for fairness but risk making them too obvious before the receipt bundle matures.
- Blink can become a late proof dump. Private/ephemeral receipts need earlier public teasers and should rarely solve WHO and HOW alone.
- Comment-driven app unlocks are overused. Later waves should diversify unlocks through profile links, screenshots, visible handles, repost chains, and content references.
- Incentive trails are often clear but sometimes too neat. Benefits should be socially legible without feeling like a formal motive card.
- Production plans must avoid tiny visual proof. Critical receipts should be readable through content, timing, framing, and repeated context.
- Endings need careful tone so the product does not encourage real-world pile-on behavior.

## Development Guidance For Wave 4

Wave 4 should develop Scenarios 02, 04, and 01. Scenario 08 should be held as a production-friendly fallback if the top three prove too creator-heavy or asset-heavy in deeper planning.
