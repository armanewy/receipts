# Collab Week Is Canceled: Report Requirements

## Report Frame

The report should feel like the player is assembling the best social theory about how the public story got shaped. The slots should ask for WHO, WHY, WHEN, HOW, and RECEIPTS, with feedback that sounds like social context comparison.

## WHO Slot Options

Correct:

- Kellan Vale.

Plausible but wrong or partial:

- Mira Sol.
- Collab Desk.
- Kellan and Mira together.
- A sponsor-adjacent launch partner.
- An unnamed fan community.

## WHY Slot Options

Correct:

- Kellan wanted sympathy traffic and a cleaner solo rollout after the joint collab started looking weak for his audience.

Partial:

- Kellan wanted attention.
- Kellan was stressed about creator numbers.
- Kellan did not want to share the launch week.

Wrong:

- Mira wanted to humiliate Kellan.
- Collab Desk wanted engagement only.
- A sponsor-adjacent partner told Kellan to cancel the collab.
- Both creators wanted a mutual fake feud.

## WHEN Slot Options

Correct:

- Kellan seeded the crop before the public Loop blowup peaked, using a planning message from three weeks earlier.

Partial:

- The Blink message was old.
- The recap account posted before Kellan's Frame story.
- Kellan had solo assets ready before the cancelation went public.

Wrong:

- Mira posted the private insult on launch day.
- The screenshot surfaced only after Kellan reacted.
- The solo rollout was improvised after the backlash.
- Collab Desk created the story after seeing Kellan's duet.

## HOW Slot Options

Correct:

- Kellan cropped an old Blink planning exchange, passed the crop through Collab Desk, then used Loop and Frame posts to make the story feel like a fresh public betrayal.

Partial:

- Old content was reframed as fresh.
- The crop removed Kellan's agreement.
- Collab Desk amplified the story.
- Kellan redirected attention to solo week.

Wrong:

- Mira leaked the screenshot to hurt Kellan.
- Collab Desk invented a fake screenshot.
- Both creators agreed to stage the feud together.
- The solo rollout caused the public story without Kellan shaping the crop.

## Receipt Slots

The report should ask the player to attach receipts for:

- WHO: CW-16 plus either CW-08 or CW-09.
- WHY: CW-10 and CW-11, with CW-18 as a substitute support receipt.
- WHEN: CW-05 and CW-14, with CW-03 as substitute support.
- HOW: CW-06, CW-13, CW-15, and CW-16.
- CONTEXT: CW-01 plus one Mira-context receipt such as CW-12 or CW-17.

## Correct Combination

Correct report:

- WHO: Kellan Vale.
- WHY: sympathy traffic and a cleaner solo-week pivot.
- WHEN: before the public Loop blowup peaked, using a three-week-old planning exchange.
- HOW: cropped old Blink context, laundered the crop through Collab Desk, then posted public reactions that guided the feed.
- Required receipts: CW-05, CW-06, CW-10, CW-11, CW-14, CW-15, CW-16.
- Strong supporting receipts: CW-03, CW-08, CW-09, CW-13, CW-18.

## Partial Combinations

Partial Kellan-without-method:

- WHO: Kellan.
- WHY: solo rollout.
- Missing: HOW receipts around the crop and Collab Desk.
- Feedback: "This explains why solo week benefited, but not how the old quote turned into the public story."

Partial old-content-only:

- WHEN: old Blink planning exchange.
- HOW: old content reframed.
- Missing: WHO and WHY.
- Feedback: "The timing is right, but the report still needs who gained from making the old line feel new."

Partial recap-account theory:

- WHO: Collab Desk.
- HOW: amplified the cropped screenshot.
- Missing: Kellan's benefit and hidden agreement.
- Feedback: "Collab Desk made the story louder, but the crop protects someone closer to the collab."

Partial mutual-staging theory:

- WHO: Kellan and Mira together.
- WHY: attention.
- Missing: Mira's still-posting message and the crop hiding Kellan's agreement.
- Feedback: "This accounts for the attention spike, but not why the crop only shields one side."

## Wrong Combinations

Mira-centered report:

- Uses CW-01, CW-04, CW-06.
- Fails because it ignores CW-14, CW-15, CW-16, and CW-17.
- Feedback: "The clipped quote makes Mira look bad, but the older planning context changes what she was talking about."

Fan-account-only report:

- Uses CW-05, CW-06, CW-07.
- Fails because it does not explain Kellan's ready solo path or the hidden reply.
- Feedback: "The recap account carried the story, but the reason for that exact crop points elsewhere."

Sponsor-pressure report:

- Uses CW-10 and CW-11.
- Fails because it makes context into the central answer.
- Feedback: "Solo-week pressure matters, but the report still needs the person who shaped the public version."

## How To Avoid Brute Force

- Require receipts across at least three apps for a complete report.
- Do not accept WHO without a matching HOW bundle.
- Do not accept Kellan as WHO unless the player also attaches private-context and timing receipts.
- Keep partial feedback specific enough to redirect comparison, but not so explicit that it names the missing receipt.
- Distinguish Kellan-only staging from mutual staging with CW-16 and CW-17.
- Make report options socially phrased, not abstract. Example: "Kellan made the old planning line look fresh" is better than "Kellan manipulated context."
