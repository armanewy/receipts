# The Empty Fifth Chair: Report Requirements

## Report Goal

The report should express a social theory built from saved receipts:

- WHO shaped the public betrayal story?
- WHY did they need the story to travel?
- WHEN did the public story break from the real order of events?
- HOW did the story get shaped across apps?
- Which RECEIPTS make the theory hold together?

## Receipt Slots

The report should ask for saved receipts that support each claim:

- WHO receipt: source-chain or crop-source support.
- WHY receipt: Cedar Room and shared-concept support.
- WHEN receipt: Frame rename timing and Loop dinner timing.
- HOW receipt: cropped/uncropped Blink chat plus source-laundering support.
- CONTEXT receipt: empty chair or group-history receipt.

## Partial Combinations

### Partial: Mina Without WHY

The player names Mina but only attaches the crop receipts.

Feedback: "This explains who had the crop, but not why Thursday mattered."

### Partial: Correct WHY With Wrong WHO

The player names Rhea or the whole group but attaches Cedar Room receipts.

Feedback: "The Cedar Room timing matters, but this does not explain who benefited from the crop traveling that way."

### Partial: Correct WHEN With Wrong HOW

The player catches the Tuesday/Wednesday mismatch but says Dax shaped the whole story.

Feedback: "The clip is out of order, but the screenshot started the pile-on before the clip took over."

### Partial: Correct HOW Without Blink Context

The player identifies source laundering but lacks the uncropped chat.

Feedback: "The screenshot traveled strangely, but the missing lines are what change its meaning."

## How To Avoid Brute Force

- Require at least one receipt for each report slot.
- Require both a timing receipt and a source/method receipt before accepting Mina as the full WHO answer.
- Keep Mina visible from the start, but make early Mina-only guesses partial unless WHY and HOW are supported.
- Let @porchwatch and Dax remain plausible partial answers so players cannot solve by picking the most central person.
- Use feedback that identifies the missing social context instead of listing required files.
