# The Sunday Soft Launch - Report Requirements

## Report Frame

The report should feel like assembling a social theory from saved receipts. The player is not making a formal accusation; they are showing which public story best explains the feed.

- Eden Vale was the hidden reason for the breakup.

- To gain sympathy and solo creator momentum before Monday's showcase while avoiding questions about delayed public timing. Correct.
- To boost @softwatcher's follower count.
- To make Eden jealous.
- To promote the queued couple carousel.

## WHEN Slot Options

- Sunday after Jace posted the breakup clip.

- Weeks earlier when Eden posted bracelet photos.

## Receipt Slots

The report should require at least five saved receipts, with one receipt type per role:

- Timeline receipt: S01-R04, S01-R07, or S01-R11.
- Jacket-context receipt: S01-R02, S01-R06, or S01-R12.
- Source-path receipt: S01-R07, S01-R08, S01-R09, or S01-R10.
- Jace-link receipt: S01-R09, S01-R10, or S01-R11.
- Benefit receipt: S01-R05 or S01-R13.

The strongest report uses seven receipts: S01-R02, S01-R04, S01-R07, S01-R09, S01-R10, S01-R11, and S01-R13.

## How To Avoid Brute Force

- Do not allow a correct WHO answer without at least one Jace-link receipt and one source-path receipt.
- Do not allow a correct HOW answer without jacket context and fan-account timing.
- Do not allow a correct WHY answer with only the showcase receipt; it must pair with delayed public timing.
- Let partial endings recognize good reads without confirming the full theory.
- Keep report options social-native and limited, but require saved receipt support before final submission.
