# Glossary

## Lurker

The player role: someone who watches, scrolls, taps deeper, compares context, and saves receipts without becoming an official participant in the drama.

## Receipt

A saved social artifact, such as a post, clip, story, DM, comment, profile detail, screenshot, or thread, that contributes apparent meaning and later actual meaning to the player's theory.

## Surface Event

The public-facing incident or social moment that pulls the player into the feed. It must be emotionally legible, shareable, and ambiguous enough to reward deeper browsing.

## Public Interpretation

The low-effort story the feed encourages. It includes the apparent actor, motive, timeline, wronged party, and supporting artifact that makes the surface story feel true.

## Actual Causal Chain

The real sequence of responsibility, pressure, action, concealment, timing, consequence, and trace left behind.

## Transformation Type

A semantic move that changes what a receipt means when more context is found, such as temporal inversion, context collapse, source laundering, selective cropping, or alt-account leakage.

## App Semantic Role

The specific job an app surface performs in the scenario, such as amplifying a public interpretation, preserving private context, revealing timeline friction, or exposing incentive.

## Contradiction Ladder

The staged path from surface belief to stronger theory: first wobble, cross-app context, timeline break, source or method link, motive or benefit, and confirmation bundle.

## Theory/Report

The structured end-state where the player assembles WHO, WHY, WHEN, HOW, and RECEIPTS into a coherent account of what happened.

## Wrong Theory

A plausible but incomplete player explanation. It should be supported by some true receipts while failing to explain the full graph.

## Doomscroll Loop

The emotional and interaction loop of scroll, notice, tap deeper, save receipt, unlock another app, compare context, form theory, and need one more receipt.

## App Disclosure/Unlock

The moment a new fictional app surface becomes available because existing content creates an in-world reason to check it.

## Source Laundering

A transformation where a claim appears credible because it has been reposted or reframed through other accounts, even though the origin is weak, biased, or hidden.

## Temporal Inversion

A transformation where the public timeline reverses cause and effect, making a reaction look like a trigger or making old content look newly meaningful.

## Context Collapse

A transformation where content meant for one audience or context is interpreted by a broader audience without the missing frame.

## Selective Cropping

A transformation where a partial image, clip, screenshot, or quote hides the surrounding material that changes its meaning.

## Alt-Account Leakage

A transformation where repeated language, visual objects, posting cadence, contacts, or metadata-like social patterns connect an account to a person indirectly.

## Soft-Launch Contradiction

A transformation where relationship hints, vague posts, partial tags, or background details conflict with the public relationship timeline.

## Incentive Trail

A chain of benefits, pressures, opportunities, or status gains that explains why someone shaped the public story.
