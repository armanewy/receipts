## Beat Design Notes

- The first three beats must make the friend group feel real enough for the empty chair to matter.
- Beats 5-7 should let the public interpretation feel plausible before destabilizing it.
- Beats 10-15 should avoid a single all-solving private message. Blink changes the crop meaning, then Frame and Threadline still carry WHY and spread.
- The loop should keep alternating between emotional artifacts and structured comparison so the experience remains browsing-first.
