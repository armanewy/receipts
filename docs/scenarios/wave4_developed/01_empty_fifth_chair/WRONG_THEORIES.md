# The Empty Fifth Chair: Wrong And Partial Theories

## Wrong Theory 1: Rhea Leaked The Chat Because She Was Jealous

### Why A Player Might Believe It

The opening Frame story names screenshots, Rhea is absent from the dinner image, and @porchwatch's cropped screenshot places Rhea near the center of the private conflict. The Loop clip makes her look emotional and isolated.

### Receipts That Support It

- S02-R01: Empty chair story.
- S02-R04: Rhea leaving the table.
- S02-R06: Cropped Blink screenshot.

### Receipts That Limit It

- S02-R02: The group highlight changed before the Threadline crop spread.
- S02-R09: The uncropped chat shows Rhea asking people not to post it.
- S02-R11: The crop aligns with Mina's view.
- S02-R12: Mina's Cedar Room benefit explains why Rhea losing trust helped Mina.

### Report Feedback

"This matches the first read of the empty chair, but it does not explain why the chair was empty before the thread took off."

### Ending Type

Full wrong ending. The feed stays simplified and Rhea remains flattened into the public role.

## Wrong Theory 2: @porchwatch Caused The Whole Spiral

### Why A Player Might Believe It

@porchwatch is the visible account that posts the cropped screenshot, gets quoted, and drives the Threadline pile-on. The account also locks replies later, which can make it look responsible for the whole mess.

### Receipts That Support It

- S02-R06: @porchwatch crop.
- S02-R07: Source-chain post.
- S02-R14: Locked replies update.

### Receipts That Limit It

- S02-R02: Frame curation started before the crop traveled.
- S02-R09: The uncropped chat changes the screenshot's meaning.
- S02-R10: Rhea had already challenged Mina about Cedar Room.
- S02-R11: The crop source points inside the group.

### Report Feedback

"@porchwatch made the screenshot travel, but the report still needs who had that crop and why it helped them."

### Ending Type

Partial ending. The player identifies the amplifier but misses the source and benefit.

## Wrong Theory 3: Dax Posted The Clip To Embarrass Rhea

### Why A Player Might Believe It

Dax's Loop clip is the most emotional artifact after the opening story. It shows Rhea leaving at the worst possible moment and keeps circulating with captions that make her look guilty.

### Receipts That Support It

- S02-R04: Reposted table clip.
- S02-R05: Comment thread around clip timing.
- S02-R14: Threadline users later ask why the clip was used out of order.

### Receipts That Limit It

- S02-R06: The cropped Blink screenshot predates the clip's strongest repost wave.
- S02-R07: The source-chain language points closer to Mina's circle.
- S02-R10: The Cedar Room pressure existed before the clip became central.
- S02-R11: The crop alignment matters more than the person who filmed dinner.

### Report Feedback

"Dax gave the feed a scene to replay. The saved receipts still point to someone else shaping the screenshot story."

### Ending Type

Partial ending. The player understands amplification but not origin.

## Wrong Theory 4: The Whole Group Quietly Agreed To Cut Rhea

### Why A Player Might Believe It

The empty-chair post presents a united table. No one immediately corrects Mina, and the old group highlight is renamed without visible protest.

### Receipts That Support It

- S02-R01: Empty chair story.
- S02-R02: "Supper Four" rename.
- S02-R13: Nia staying quiet about the Cedar Room invite.

### Receipts That Limit It

- S02-R07: The crop source chain narrows toward Mina's language and circle.
- S02-R09: Mina's removed lines drive the private conflict.
- S02-R10: Rhea addresses Mina directly about the shared deck.
- S02-R12: Mina, not the whole group, receives the solo opportunity.

### Report Feedback

"The group helped the public story sit there, but the strongest receipts point to one person setting it up."

### Ending Type

Partial ending. The player understands social pressure but misses individual agency.

## Wrong Theory 5: Cedar Room Pushed Mina To Rebrand Solo

### Why A Player Might Believe It

The venue announcement creates the clearest benefit. It is easy to overread the platform-like context and assume the opportunity provider wanted the friend group simplified.

### Receipts That Support It

- S02-R12: Cedar Room announcement.
- S02-R15: Mina's early profile update.
- S02-R03: Shared supper-club concept history.

### Receipts That Limit It

- S02-R06: The crop is a social artifact, not a venue action.
- S02-R09: Mina's private chat lines are removed from the public crop.
- S02-R11: The crop aligns with Mina's view.
- S02-R13: Friends respond to access pressure, but no venue account asks for a pile-on.

### Report Feedback

"Cedar Room explains the pressure, not who turned the group chat into the public story."

### Ending Type

Wrong ending if chosen as WHO, partial if used only as WHY context.

## Design Notes For Wrong Theories

- Every wrong theory should be supported by at least one real receipt.
- Wrong theories should fail because they cannot account for another receipt category, not because they are random.
- Feedback should sound like social context: "this explains the clip, not the crop" or "this explains the pressure, not the timing."
- The player should be able to revise from a wrong theory by returning to saved receipts, not by being handed a direct answer.
