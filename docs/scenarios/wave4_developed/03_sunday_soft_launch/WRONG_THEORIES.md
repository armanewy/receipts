# The Sunday Soft Launch - Wrong Theories

## Wrong Theory 1: Mara Overlapped With Sol

### Why A Player Might Believe It

The orange jacket is a strong visual symbol, the cropped Loop clip frames it as romantic, and early comments treat Sol's studio presence as suspicious.

### Receipts That Support It

- S01-R01: cropped Studio 6 exit clip.
- S01-R06: Sol's studio story.
- S01-R12: Sol's jacket snap if read without context.

### Receipts That Limit Or Disprove It

- S01-R02 shows the fuller rehearsal context.
- S01-R11 shows the breakup was already private before Sunday.
- S01-R07 and S01-R09 show the public story was shaped through timing and amplification, not caused by a new relationship.

### Report Feedback

"This follows the jacket read, but it leaves the Thursday timing and pre-clip thread unexplained."

### Ending Type

Full wrong ending. The feed stays focused on the wrong symbol and the player misses who benefited from the timing.

## Wrong Theory Design Notes

- Each wrong theory should explain at least one real receipt.
- None should be silly or arbitrary.
- Wrong-theory feedback should sound like social interpretation, not a formal correction.
- The best wrong theories should teach the player which missing context matters: timing, source path, benefit, or jacket meaning.
