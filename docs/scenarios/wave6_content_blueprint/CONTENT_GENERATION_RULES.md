# Content Generation Rules

## Scenario Lock

All future content must preserve the selected MVP scenario truth:

- Mina Saye shaped the public betrayal story.
- Rhea Vale did not leak the chat directly.
- Mina's pressure came from the Cedar Room solo supper-series opportunity and the shared Supper Five concept.
- The public Frame cut happened before the @porchwatch crop spread.
- Dax's Loop table clip happened on Tuesday and was later framed as if it responded to the public leak.
- The Blink crop hides Mina's removed lines and Rhea's de-escalating context.

Do not introduce a new responsible party or change the causal chain.

## Semantics First

- Start from receipt meaning before writing wording.
- Every critical post, comment, story, clip, or DM must map to a receipt ID or serve a named atmospheric role.
- Apparent meaning and actual meaning must both be intentional.
- Atmospheric content must not contradict the true timeline.
- Red herrings must be true-but-incomplete.

## Writing Style

- Keep posts short and social-native.
- Avoid overly literary phrasing.
- Avoid exposition dumps.
- Let comments feel varied, messy, and partially informed.
- Do not make commenters sound like hint systems.
- Do not use direct confessions.
- Private messages should feel incomplete and human, not like summaries.

## Product Vocabulary

Use:

- receipts
- posts
- clips
- stories
- DMs
- comments
- profiles
- source
- thread
- theory
- report
- WHO / WHY / WHEN / HOW

Avoid product-facing restricted vocabulary and procedural framing.

## App And IP Rules

- Use Loop, Frame, Threadline, and Blink only.
- Do not use real platform names, exact UI, real marks, or real app branding.
- All people, venues, handles, and communities are fictional.
- No real people, public figures, lookalikes, or real event references.
- Do not use real venue names for Cedar Room inspiration.

## Receipt Integrity

- Every required receipt must remain represented.
- Receipt IDs must remain stable.
- Do not collapse multiple required receipts into one all-solving artifact.
- S02-R11 must be a bundle of readable supports, not tiny-pixel proof.
- Blink must reframe context but should not answer everything alone.
- Cedar Room should explain pressure, not act as a faceless responsible party.

## Safety And Tone

- Keep the scenario fictional and contained.
- Avoid encouraging real-world pile-on behavior.
- The ending should show the feed softening and splintering, not mobilizing against Mina.
- Rhea should be imperfect but not flattened into the public role.
- Mina should feel socially human, not cartoonishly strategic.
